## 1. Findings

### 1.1 The three forms, and when each is used

| Form | What it is | When practitioners use it | Grade / source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a) Amendment, changes only** | A short instrument that names the original agreement and then sets out numbered operative changes: *"Clause X is deleted and replaced with…"*, *"…is amended and restated in its entirety to read as follows"*, *"a new Clause Y is inserted"*. It ends with *"except as amended, all other terms remain in full force and effect"*. The original stays the operative document, as modified | The default for a small, targeted change. If the changes are few, an amendment is usually enough | **S** https://www.contracts365.com/blog/the-definitive-guide-to-amendment-addendum-restatement ; https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/amending-and-restating-a-contract.html . Clause wording: **S** https://lawinsider.com/clause/amend-and-restate |
| **(b) Amended and Restated Agreement (A&R)** | The whole agreement reissued with every change folded in. It supersedes the prior text from a stated effective date. It names the original and every prior amendment, and states that the agreement **continues** (it is not terminated) and that accrued rights are preserved | When many amendments have accumulated, the changes are widespread, or the document has become hard to follow. No source gives a fixed number | **S** https://www.sprintlaw.co.uk/articles/amended-and-restated-agreements-in-the-uk-when-and-how-to-use-them ; https://fynk.com/en/clauses/amended-and-restated-agreement/ |
| **(c) Amendment plus a conformed copy** | Form (a) is the signed, operative instrument. A **consolidated text** showing the cumulative effect is attached or circulated, either clean or marked (underlined additions, struck deletions). That copy is expressly **not binding**, and the amendment's operative words control | Common in finance and regulated contracts, where people need to work from the current text without re-signing the whole agreement | **P** (filed instrument): Culp Inc. 10-K Ex-10.1, *"Conformed Copy – Credit Agreement…"*: *"This Document Is an Unofficial Composite That Reflects the Cumulative Net Effect…"*, *"Provided Solely for Ease of Review and Convenience."*, *"This Is Not a Legally Binding Document."* https://www.bamsec.com/companies/723603/culp-inc/credit-agreements . **S** (search snippet only, not opened): the UK regulator ORR publishes Network Rail's consolidated agreements *"for reference purposes only to provide an overview of the current terms"* https://orr.gov.uk/search?page=5 . **S** for the marked-copy wording (*"amended by adding the text marked with double-underlining and deleting the struck through text"*): https://contracts.justia.com/contract-clauses/amendment-to-credit-agreement/658955 |

**Is there a threshold?** No source found states a numeric rule. The criteria are qualitative: how many amendments exist,
how widespread the changes are, and whether the text can still be followed. **S** (sources above). Any number LegalMind uses is
a product choice, not a legal rule (§2.3).

**The conformed copy is a reading aid.** It is typically labelled as *"Conformed copy through Amendment No. N"*
(**S** https://lawinsider.com/contracts/85frKTaqnMP). Where it and the signed amendments disagree, the signed amendments
control (**P**, the Culp disclaimer above; **S** the Lexis+ glossary https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/glossary/conformed-copy ,
which is paywalled and seen only as a snippet).

### 1.2 What an amendment document contains (practice consensus)

Each element below appears in the sources. Where the Constitution or the MSA already requires an element, that is marked **R**.

| # | Element | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title and number**, e.g. *"Amendment No. 2 to the Master Services Agreement"* | **S** (Culp / LawInsider titling); **R** Constitution §31.12 *"Each amendment should reference the version/date of the agreement it amends"* (LegalMind Rule) |
| 2 | **Parties**, identical to the original's legal entities (or a successor stated as such) | **S** Fynk; **I**: a party change is a novation, not an amendment (§1.4) |
| 3 | **Recitals**: the original agreement by name and date, its version, and **every prior amendment** by number and date | **S** Sprintlaw (A&R must identify the original and prior amendments); **R** §31.12 |
| 4 | **Definitions**: capitalised terms take the original's meaning unless redefined | **S** LawInsider defined-terms clauses https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/amended-and-restated-defined-terms |
| 5 | **Numbered operative changes**, each naming the exact clause and using one verb: *deleted* / *deleted and replaced with* / *inserted after* / *amended to read*. Changes are limited to the named clauses | **S** LawInsider; **R** §31.12 *"Should be limited to the specific clause(s) identified in the amendment; should not silently override unrelated terms"* (LegalMind Rule) |
| 6 | **Effective date**, which may differ from the signature date, and whether it is retrospective | **R** §31.12 (LegalMind Rule); **S** the Cone Mills amendment: changes effective *"as of the date first set forth above"* https://contracts.justia.com/companies/cone-mills-corp-74682/contract/927762 |
| 7 | **No other change / ratification**: *"Except as expressly amended, the Agreement remains in full force and effect"* | **S** (all practice sources); **R** §31.12 *"The amendment prevails only as to the specific terms it amends; all other original terms remain unchanged"* (Company-approved) |
| 8 | **Precedence**: the amendment prevails over the original **only as to the terms it amends**. References to "this Agreement" are read as the Agreement as amended | **R** §31.12 (Company-approved); **S** Sprintlaw |
| 9 | **Conformed-copy disclaimer** (only when one is attached): the copy is for reference, and the amendment controls | **P** Culp |
| 10 | **Governing law and disputes**, by reference to the original | **I** (keeps one forum; the MSA's own clause governs) |
| 11 | **Counterparts and electronic signature** | **R** LeapSwitch MSA §21.10 *"may be executed in any number of counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original"*; IT Act s.5, s.10A (§1.4) |
| 12 | **Signatures by authorised representatives** of each party, with name, designation and date | **R** MSA §21.9 *"This Agreement may not be modified except by an instrument in writing signed by the duly authorized representatives of the Parties."*; **R** §31.12 *"Must be signed by an authorized representative of each party"*; authority under Companies Act 2013 s.21 is already recorded in report 02 (**P**) |
| 13 | **Stamping status** (paid or not, the article, the certificate reference), recorded by a person | **P** Maharashtra Stamp Act s.17, s.34 (§1.4); report 10 R-11 |

The LeapSwitch MSA itself expects this instrument. Change requests are *"captured in an addendum or amendment to this
Agreement, which shall have full contractual effect"* (§6.1.4), and §6.5.4 says the same (**R**, `legal-docs/MSA.pdf`).

### 1.4 The Indian-law angle (primary where reached)

* **Contract Act 1872 s.62:** *"If the parties to a contract agree to substitute a new contract for it, or to rescind or
  alter it, the original contract, need not be performed."* **P** (India Code copy,
  `legal-docs/Indian_Laws_and_Acts/Indian_Contract_Act_1872_indiacode.pdf`; https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2187).
  Illustration (a) is novation by a change of debtor. The section covers three different things:
  * an **amendment** is an agreed *alteration*: the contract continues, as altered;
  * a **substitution** is a novation: the old contract is discharged and a new one takes its place;
  * a **rescission** ends the contract.
  Commentators say the change must be bilateral, and that a novation needs a change going to the **root** of the contract,
  not a minor variation (**S** https://www.casemine.com/in/column/section-62-of-the-indian-contract-act,-1872-–-contemporary-jurisprudential-analysis/view).
  **Consequence for an A&R (I, lawyer question):** a full restatement looks like a substitution. It should therefore say
  expressly that it amends and continues the original, does not novate it, and preserves accrued rights, liabilities and
  any unpaid fees. Otherwise a counterparty could argue that the old obligations were discharged.
* **Evidence:** BSA 2023 s.95 bars oral evidence that varies written terms, but allows proof of a distinct later oral
  agreement to modify them. That is the evidential reason to write every change down (**P**, recorded in report 10 §3.1).
* **Stamp duty (Maharashtra; LeapSwitch is in Pune).**
  * s.2(l): an "instrument" includes every document by which a right or liability *"is, or purports to be created,
    transferred, limited, extended, extinguished or recorded"*.
  * s.17: stamp before or at execution, or on the next working day.
  * s.34: an unstamped instrument is inadmissible until duty and penalty are paid.
  * Sources for these three: **P** IGR consolidated text, https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/SB_PUBLICATION/DATA/Schedule/_STAMP_ACT%20amended%2003.6.2022.pdf ; already verified in reports 02 and 10.
  * An unstamped instrument is not void. Non-stamping is a curable defect (*In re Interplay*, 2023 INSC 1066, seven judges; **S**, report 02).
  * **Schedule I, Article 5(h)(B)** (an agreement "not otherwise provided for"): Maharashtra Ordinance XII of 2024, dated
    14 October 2024, substituted *"Five hundred rupees"* for *"One hundred rupees"* (**P**, Gazette English translation,
    https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/maharashtra/2024/Ord12of2024MH.pdf). Report 10 records it as made
    permanent by Mah. Act IX of 2025 (**S**, VL-30).
  * **Article 5(h)(A)(iv)** covers an agreement creating an obligation "having monetary value": 0.1% of the amount agreed
    up to ₹10 lakh (minimum ₹100), 0.2% above (**P**, PRS text, recorded in report 02).
  * **Inference, lawyer question:**
    * An amendment that changes only legal terms is plausibly a ₹500 Article 5(h)(B) instrument.
    * An amendment that changes fees or value may fall under 5(h)(A)(iv).
    * An **A&R reissues the whole agreement**, so it may be charged as a fresh instrument on the full contract value. Whether
      credit is given for duty already paid on the original is not settled by anything read here.
    * This is a real cost argument for preferring form (c) over form (b).
  * s.4 relief (several instruments, one transaction) is limited to development agreements, sale, lease, mortgage and
    settlement, so it does not obviously cover an MSA chain (**P**, report 02).
  * Other States: Indian Stamp Act 1899 s.35 has the same inadmissibility bar (**P**, report 02).
* **E-signing.**
  * IT Act s.5: *"Where any law provides that information or any other matter shall be authenticated by affixing the
    signature or any document shall be signed or bear the signature of any person, then, notwithstanding anything contained
    in such law, such requirement shall be deemed to have been satisfied, if such information or matter is authenticated by
    means of electronic signature affixed in such manner as may be prescribed by the Central Government."* **P**
    (`legal-docs/…/IT_Act_2000_indiacode.pdf`).
  * s.3A and the Second Schedule cover reliable techniques (Aadhaar e-KYC e-sign) (**P**).
  * s.10A: a contract formed electronically *"shall not be deemed to be unenforceable solely on the ground that such
    electronic form or means was used"* (**P**).
  * s.1(4) excludes the **First Schedule** documents. As amended by S.O. 4720(E) of 26 September 2022, these are:
    * a negotiable instrument, other than a cheque, demand promissory note or bill issued in favour of or endorsed by an
      RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA/NHB-regulated entity;
    * a power of attorney, with a similar carve-out;
    * a trust;
    * a will.
    Serial 5, contracts for the sale of immovable property, was omitted (**P**, the India Code text and its footnotes).
  * **A services MSA amendment is not on the First Schedule.** E-signing it is within the Act (**P** reading of the
    Schedule).
  * The Maharashtra Stamp Act's definitions of "signed" and "document" follow the IT Act, so an e-signed amendment is still
    a stampable instrument (**P**, report 10 §3.3).

## 2. Recommendation for LegalMind (RECOMMENDATION, not decided; amendment wording needs the lawyer)

### 2.1 Generate form (c) by default: a changes-only amendment plus a non-binding conformed copy

* **The signed instrument is the changes-only amendment (form a).** Its reasons:
  * It matches MSA §21.9 and Constitution §31.12, which limits an amendment to the clauses it names and gives it
    precedence only over those terms.
  * It keeps the parent as the operative document, so there is no novation question under s.62.
  * It is the cheapest stamping position (I, lawyer question).
  * It is what the Ironclad and SpotDraft data models expect: one parent, no amending amendments.
* **Generate a conformed copy beside it, every time.** LegalMind already holds the parent clause by clause.
  * It is rendered from the parent plus every signed amendment in order, and labelled *"Conformed copy through Amendment
    No. N, for reference only, not signed. The signed Agreement and amendments control."* (the Culp wording pattern, P).
  * It offers a clean view and a marked view.
  * It is never sent for signature, never stamped, and never becomes a contract version that the evaluator treats as
    executed.
  * It is the product's **"current effective terms" view**: the text equivalent of Ironclad's metadata rollup, which no
    vendor reviewed here offers as merged text.
* **The amendment's required contents** are the 13 elements in §1.2. These are template slots. The operative change
  sentences use the four fixed verbs (deleted / deleted and replaced / inserted / amended to read) and quote the parent's
  clause number. The clause prose of each change comes from the accepted change or the lawyer's fallback, never from the
  model (rule 7, `AM-25`).

### 2.2 Offer form (b), an Amended and Restated MSA, only on a person's choice, prompted by a threshold

The A&R is a different instrument with different consequences: a possible novation reading and possibly fresh stamp duty.
So LegalMind **suggests** it and never **switches** to it on its own. Prompt *"consider an Amended and Restated MSA"* when
any of these is true:

1. **three or more** signed amendments already exist on the parent, so the next would be Amendment No. 4; or
2. the proposed amendment touches **more than about a quarter** of the parent's top-level clauses; or
3. it changes a **party, the Term structure, or the governing law and seat**. Those are root-of-the-contract changes, close
   to s.62 substitution, so the lawyer should decide whether a restatement or a novation is the right instrument.

These thresholds are **product heuristics (I)**. No source states a number. They belong in configuration, not code, and the
lawyer should confirm them. When an A&R is generated, it must carry recitals naming the original and every amendment, a
continuation and no-novation clause, and an accrued-rights clause (§1.4). Its stamping is flagged as a lawyer question.

### 2.3 Data model (I)

* Keep report 10 **R-9**:
  * an Amendment is its own contract record with **exactly one parent**;
  * an amendment cannot amend another amendment;
  * there are no cycles;
  * amendments are numbered sequentially per parent.
* An A&R becomes a **new version of the parent**, with the superseded text and all amendments preserved and marked
  superseded (rule 17).
* The conformed copy is **derived output**, rebuilt on demand. It is never stored as an executed document.
* Each amendment carries a stamping-status field that a person fills in (report 10 R-11). LegalMind computes no duty.

### 2.4 For the lawyer (§16(b))

1. Confirm the Article 5 sub-clause for a legal-terms-only amendment, a fee-changing amendment and an A&R, and whether an A&R gets credit for duty already paid.
2. Approve the amendment template wording, including the no-other-change, precedence, conformed-copy disclaimer and no-novation clauses.
3. Confirm or replace the §2.2 thresholds.
