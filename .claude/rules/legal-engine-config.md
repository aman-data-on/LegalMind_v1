# The evaluator, Company Standards, the corpus and source material

Before naming any state value read [DECISION_STATE_MODEL.md](../../docs/02-legal-domain/DECISION_STATE_MODEL.md):
Mapping State, Classification, Rule Outcome, Legal Decision and Review Lifecycle are five axes
that never share a field (`AMBIGUOUS` means three different things on three layers). Before
touching mapping or extraction read `AM-54` (and `AM-60`, which removed its in-family gate).

## Company positions

- **The Legal Constitution governs company positions** (`AM-43`, AB-14). Canonical:
  [LEGAL_CONSTITUTION_L1.17.md](../../docs/02-legal-domain/LEGAL_CONSTITUTION_L1.17.md) (`AM-313`
  as `AM-325` records it: L1.16 with §9 taking a Party's obligation to pay fees and charges due
  outside the cap; earlier L1.10 `AM-59` → L1.11 `AM-115` → L1.12 `AM-129` → L1.13 `AM-131` →
  L1.14 `AM-163` → L1.15 `AM-243` → L1.16 `AM-300` (one row's NDA counterparty name redacted);
  L1.5 superseded). The live website is authoritative for the published Privacy Policy, TOS,
  SLA and AUP (owner 2026-10-09; Ask reads them daily, `AM-130`). Its positions reach the
  evaluator **only** as ratified standard files; since `AM-79` it is also a retrievable
  `knowledge_items` source for Ask. Everything in it is ratified except what it disclaims itself
  (`AM-73`): **§31.6a stays unratified**. `constitution.basis` records provenance and never
  suppresses an answer.
- **Standards live in exactly one place:** `backend/config/company_standards/*.json` — 99 files,
  92 active, 7 retired `DEPRECATED` by `AM-65` (counted 2026-11-09 after `AM-322`; **count before quoting**).
  Drafts under `proposed/` are read by nothing until ratified. Fixtures reference a standard by
  `company_standard_ref` — never copy values into a fixture. A standard file is configuration,
  not specification; every position quotes its Constitution section verbatim and nothing is
  authored beyond it (rules 7/21, `AM-59` r6, `AM-72`). §31.9–§31.12 standards are
  generated by `tools/generate_section31_standards.py` (`--check` fails CI on drift).
- **Liability:** `LIABILITY-MSA-001` and `LIABILITY-TOS-001` are both **12 months of total fees**
  (`FEES_PAID`). History: MSA was 6 months of affected-service fees from `MSA.pdf` §17.2 until
  `AM-43` r4 (2026-09-08); that template now fails closed against its own position.
  `FEES_PAID` and `FEES_PAID_FOR_AFFECTED_SERVICES` stay distinct — never add either to
  `comparable_bases` or a conversion rule. A LeapSwitch outbound limitation is never converted
  into a counterparty acceptance policy without the owner (45B.4).
- **Applicability is by CONTENT** (`AM-51`): every pinned standard is mapped first; a standard
  applies when the document confirms its clause or belongs to a detected family; MISSING only
  inside a detected family; the document type (Step 6 values, `domain/document_types.py`,
  extended by `AM-72`) is one optional signal, never a gate. An NDA with no liability clause gets
  no liability Finding; one that carries a cap is measured. Type storage is JSONB (Q2=A; C-13 open).
- **SLA:** service credits are a remedy, not a liability cap (owner 2026-08-20, L-13); never
  create an SLA-typed liability standard. Pinned by `test_an_sla_is_never_measured_against_a_liability_requirement`.
- MSA §17.2 and §17.7 govern one scope and contradict → `CONFLICT` (fixture `DOC-LIAB-04`).

## The Legal Rule — zero tolerance, plus one approved favour form

- **Zero tolerance** (manager 2026-08-19, owner-approved and wired 2026-08-20): MATCH →
  `ACCEPTABLE`; any DEVIATION → `UNACCEPTABLE` → a Legal Decision. Every standard carries
  `legal_rule.configuration = {"deviation_outcome": "UNACCEPTABLE", "unlimited_outcome": "UNACCEPTABLE"}`.
  No tolerance band exists **by policy**; never ask for or infer a threshold. `AM-33` withdrew
  the band FORM: `acceptable_max`/`approval_required_above` fail closed to a human.
- **Second approved form, `AM-239`** (2026-10-30): `favourable_outcome: ACCEPTABLE` — a deviation
  on the side the standard names (`favourable_direction`, from a source) is ACCEPTABLE and stays a
  DEVIATION; only where LeapSwitch is the provider (MSA/TOS/SLA/ORDER_FORM/AMENDMENT), never a
  one-party cap, never with `favourable_counsel_check`. Read `AM-239` before touching it.
- Unruled deviations go to a human: `UNRULED_DEVIATION_REQUIRES_DECISION` in
  `evaluation/workflow.py` widens D-3.5 (F-4 allows widening only). `NOT_APPLICABLE` is the
  fail-closed Rule Outcome (Step 20 r4) — **never add a fifth `RuleOutcome`** (45B.26).
- Keep `classification` (what a provision is) and `rule_outcome` (what Legal should do) apart;
  `load_fixture` and `test_no_fixture_asserts_an_acceptance_policy` enforce it.

## Evaluators

Three exist (`evaluation/registry.py`): `NUMERIC_COMPARISON` and `PRESENCE` (`AM-16`), and
`CATEGORICAL` (`AM-148`). A fourth is an additive amendment, not a code change. No specific
Requirement beyond `LIABILITY-001` is required by a lock — Termination, Indemnification,
Governing Law etc. are configuration ([EDGE_CASES](../../docs/04-analysis-engine/EDGE_CASES/)).

## Preserve the examples

The specification's worked examples (MATCH vs DEVIATION, RESOLVED ≠ MATCH, APPROVAL_REQUIRED,
customization, traceability, snapshots, liability, multiple clauses, conflicting provisions) are
deliberate. Never delete or "clean up" one; add new specs with worked examples in the same style.
